control and did not ask what the scroll had moved away from. The header is inside
the same ScrollView; there is one scroller, not a fixed header over a scrolling
body.

53 on `media-page-1` after a swipe. Its cause was never established — page 1 was
measured to exist at x=304, one page right — so it is recorded as **a flake that
resolved itself**, not as fixed. If it fails again the swipe is the thing to
look at.
