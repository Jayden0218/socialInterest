## Asserted through the service, not the view hierarchy

The screen assertions in a flow prove a person can reach and operate the UI. What
makes this a *result* is the API's own request log, which the run checks after
the journeys — and which no view assertion can satisfy.

**005/US1 — rating and reviewing a place:**

```
2 "method":"PUT","path":"/v1/places/:placeId/rating","status":200
4 "method":"GET","path":"/v1/places/:placeId/reviews","status":200
```

Plus two checks the script makes directly: the review written on the device is
readable on the place page (`Rated from a real device.` present in
`GET /v1/places/{id}/reviews`), and that response carries an `author` — the
hydration check, because six surfaces in this codebase shipped returning raw
candidate rows.

**005/US3 — the whole group lifecycle:**

```
1 "method":"POST","path":"/v1/conversations/groups","status":201
1 "method":"POST","path":"/v1/conversations/:conversationId/participants","status":204
1 "method":"POST","path":"/v1/conversations/:conversationId/leave","status":204
4 "method":"POST","path":"/v1/conversations/:conversationId/messages","status":201
```

Create, add, message, leave — and after leaving, `GET /v1/conversations` no
longer returns the group for that person (FR-021, asked of the server rather
than inferred from a list the app had just re-fetched).

The rest of the product is unchanged and green alongside it: 9 × `POST /v1/posts`
201, 9 × `POST /v1/media/uploads` 201, a comment 201, a report 201, a save 204, a
place created 201 and followed 204, `PATCH /v1/me` 200, an accept 204.

## What it took: three runs, and only one of the three failures was worth a run

- **Run 32 (17/19)** — both new flows failed. `20-rate-place` chained
  `16-place-page`, whose follow toggle had already fired ten minutes earlier, so
  the chained tap unfollowed and 16's own assertion correctly failed. A toggle is
  not idempotent, and flows share one server.
- **Run 33 (18/19)** — `20-rate-place` fixed and passing. `21-group-chat` failed
  on `tab-chats`, which does not exist on a pushed screen: `App` renders the tab
  bar only at the root of the stack. That run's log already showed
  `POST /v1/conversations/groups` 201 and `.../participants` 204 — the product
  had done everything asked of it.
- **Run 34 (19/19)** — after `nav-back`, and after giving a group's open button
  an identity (`open-group-<slug>`) instead of `open-conversation-.*`, which
  matches whichever row renders first and by then would have opened a pair.

**Two of those three failures were navigation facts a browser settles in three
seconds.** `005/J-21` in `apps/e2e/browser/navigation.spec.ts` now drives the
whole flow — create, send, back, find by name, open the group, leave — and
asserts both explicitly:

```
expect(await page.locator(id('tab-chats')).count()).toBe(0);
expect(await openGroup.count()).toBe(1);
```

2,997ms. That check exists because I spent two 25-minute device runs learning
what it now tells me for free, which is the lesson this repository already had
written down and I reproduced anyway.
