## The design decision Phase D turned around

The plan (T181, T184) had every `toCandidate` construction populate an `authorPrivacy` field
on the candidate. **It was implemented and then removed.**

Privacy is a property of the AUTHOR, not of the post, so carrying it on a candidate means
either denormalising it into every index row — a flip would then need a re-index, which
FR-044 and 001/FR-017 forbid — or asking all thirteen post surfaces to populate a field,
where the cost of forgetting one is that a private account stays public on that surface.
That is the "declared half with no other half" failure this whole feature exists to end, in
the one place where its consequence is a privacy leak.

`RelationshipCache.isPrivateAccount` resolves it inside the boundary instead: memoised per
request beside the follow and block reads, consulted only on a `public` post for a viewer who
is not the author, and **failing closed** if the read throws. The evidence that this was the
right way round is that `privacy-is-not-per-surface.spec.ts`'s allow-list got SHORTER — and
**no read path is on it**.

### Measured, and fixed

Measured in a browser at the emulator's OWN viewport — the AVD's colour buffers
are 320×616 — not at a round number.

| Flow | Failed on | Measured | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| `34-private-account` | `account-privacy` not visible | it sits at **y=667** | scroll to it; `assertVisible` does not scroll |
| `35-appeal` | `open-moderation-notices` not visible | **y=967** | same |
| `09-report-and-block` | `report-reason-0` not found | reason ends at **164**, `block-person` starts at **852** — 688 apart on a 616 screen | choose the reason BEFORE scrolling to the block control |
| `12-interest-from-feed` | the seeded caption not visible | — | scroll to it: **the feed is ranked and exploration re-samples per call**, so "in the first screenful of one draw" is not a property the product offers |

**34 and 35 are my flows and the mistake is one I had already been warned about.**
`safety-fit.spec.ts` passes both controls because I gave those cases
`allowScroll`, which asks *is this reachable*. Maestro's `assertVisible` asks *is
this on screen*. Two different questions, and I wrote the guard for the weaker
one while the flow asked the stronger one. Edit profile scrolls on a measurement
(007/T051) and its primary action is in the header at y=80, so a control at y=667
is correct product behaviour — the FLOW had to move.

**09 is the more interesting one**, because it is not new code at all: the
ordering has always been wrong and the sheet grew until it stopped getting away
with it. The flow scrolled down to assert the block control and then tapped a
reason that scrolling had taken off the top.
